    Step one — place the order

    Basket

    Open the outlet, then work down this list ticking as you add. Grab can't take a basket from another site, so this is the next best thing: everything totalled, in their wording, with a copy button per line. One line here = one thing you add in Grab — a set's drink is chosen inside the set, so it's on the set's line rather than a line of its own.

    Step two — check it off

    Everyone's order, line by line

    One row per person, in the order they replied. Tick each row as you add it to the cart — the ticks are just for you, on this device.

    Step one · carried over from the Order Sheet

    What the celebration cost

    The total and the date fill themselves from the orders on the Order Sheet; type the real charge over the top when the GrabFood receipt lands. The celebration is funded at a fixed rate per attending head — spend under it and the whole receipt is claimable, spend over and the page claims the budget and says so.

    What the money went on

    The order, itemised

    One GrabFood order, one receipt — so Yeeflow gets a single Claim Details row. This is what sits behind that row: every item, every price. Print it or paste it in as the supporting detail, and keep it with the receipt.
